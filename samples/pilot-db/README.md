# Pilot-DB databank samples

These are the 42 databases of the [Pilot-DB databank](https://pilot-db.sourceforge.net/databank.htm),
converted from Pilot-DB's PalmOS `.pdb` format to AnDB `.andb` files and grouped as the databank grouped
them: English, German and French. Add one with **Import database…** in AnDB, or open it from a file
manager or email.

The databank listed 2 databases on both the English and German pages; they are kept once, in
`german/`, and listed under both here.

What carries over: fields (Text, Note, Checkbox, Integer, Date, Time and List with its choices), the
records in Pilot-DB's stored order, and Pilot-DB's list views with their columns. Repeated field names
are numbered ("Ch", "Ch 2"), and stray spaces around values are trimmed.

## Credits and terms

Each database is the work of the author credited below, as listed on the Pilot-DB databank, where they
were offered for download to Pilot-DB users. **They are not part of AnDB and are not covered by AnDB's
licence (GPL-3.0)**; no licence was stated for them. They are included here only as samples of what
Pilot-DB users made. If you are an author or rights holder and would like a database removed or credited
differently, please open an issue and it will be done.

The data dates from around 2001–2002 and has not been updated, so treat it as historical.
